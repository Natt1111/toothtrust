# X-Ray Description — Case 02

**Tooth**: #8 (maxillary right central incisor)  
**View**: Periapical radiograph, anterior maxilla

## Findings

- Large periapical radiolucency, approximately 5mm in diameter at the apex of tooth #8
- Root morphology: single straight root, no curvature, adequate length
- No vertical root fracture visible; root outline is intact bilaterally
- No lateral radiolucency (no lateral canal pathology or vertical defect)
- Coronal structure: no existing crown; adequate tooth structure visible above the alveolar crest
- Adjacent tooth #9: periapical area within normal limits; prior RCT evident, appears successful
- Alveolar bone: localized periapical bone loss corresponding to the radiolucency; no generalized bone loss

## Radiographic Interpretation

Large symptomatic periapical lesion on tooth #8. Absence of vertical root fracture and presence of intact restorable tooth structure makes endodontic treatment (primary RCT or retreatment) a viable first option per AAE guidelines. The proposed treatment plan (extraction + implant) skips the endodontic option entirely. AAE position: when no fracture is present and the tooth is restorable, retreatment should be offered as Option A before extraction.
