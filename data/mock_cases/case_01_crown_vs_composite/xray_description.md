# X-Ray Description — Case 01

**Tooth**: #19 (mandibular left first molar)  
**View**: Bitewing radiograph, left posterior

## Findings

- Occlusal radiolucency consistent with caries, estimated depth: into dentin, not approaching pulp
- Lesion involves approximately 30% of the occlusal surface
- No interproximal component (no mesial or distal extension visible)
- Cuspal structure intact — no evidence of undermining
- No fracture lines
- Periapical region: within normal limits, no radiolucency
- Alveolar bone level: normal, no horizontal or vertical bone loss
- Existing restorations on adjacent teeth: #18 occlusal composite (intact), #20 unremarkable

## Radiographic Interpretation

Single-surface occlusal caries, ICDAS score 4–5 (established lesion into dentin, not deep). No features that elevate restorability concern. No pulpal or periapical pathology. Tooth is a strong candidate for a direct single-surface composite restoration (D2391) with no clinical indication for full-coverage crown at this stage.
